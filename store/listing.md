## Description
Tomatime lives in your menu bar and keeps your focus sessions honest.

Pick a tomato:
• Green tomato: 20 minutes
• Red tomato: 25 minutes
• Golden tomato: 45 minutes
• Diamond tomato: 60 minutes

Focus sessions never run past an hour. When time's up, a pulsing red screen covers your Mac so you can't miss it. Choose what's next:
• 5 min break, then next session: rest, and the next tomato starts by itself
• 5 or 10 minute break
• 1 more minute: finish your thought (never past the one-hour limit)

Also:
• Live countdown in the menu bar
• Pause or reset anytime
• Optional Open at Login
• No account, no tracking, no data collected

Uses Reduce Motion? The break screen stays steady instead of pulsing.

## Review notes
Tomatime is a menu-bar app: no Dock icon and no window. After launch, click the tomato icon with the countdown in the menu bar (top right).

1. Pick a session length in the menu. Green tomato (20 min) is the shortest.
2. Click Start. When the countdown reaches 0:00, a translucent red screen covers all displays with four buttons: "5 min break, then next session", "5 min break", "10 min break" and "1 more minute". Any button dismisses it. "1 more minute" is hidden once a session would pass 60 minutes.
3. "Open at Login" is off by default. It registers a login item (SMAppService) only when the user turns it on in the menu.

The app collects no data and makes no network requests.
