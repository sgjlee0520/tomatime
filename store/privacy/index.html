Tomatime — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08
Developer: Songgun Lee
Contact: yagurootajum@gmail.com
App: Tomatime for Mac (com.sgjlee.tomatime)

Summary

Tomatime collects no data. It has no account, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no third-party SDKs. It makes no network requests.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your chosen tomato size (20, 25, 45 or 60 minutes), saved in the app's own storage so it is remembered next time. It is deleted when you delete the app.
  • Open at Login, only if you turn it on. macOS keeps this setting, and you can turn it off in the menu or in System Settings → General → Login Items.

Children

Tomatime collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new policy is posted on this page.

Support

  • Tomatime lives in the menu bar (top right of the screen); it has no Dock icon or window. Click the tomato and countdown to open its menu.
  • Pick a tomato: green 20 min, red 25 min, golden 45 min, diamond 60 min. Then click Start.
  • When a session ends, a red screen covers your displays until you choose a break or one more minute. Focus sessions never run longer than 60 minutes.
  • If you use Reduce Motion (System Settings → Accessibility → Display), the red screen stays steady instead of pulsing.
