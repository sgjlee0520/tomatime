Four tomatoes, or type your own length, up to 8 hours. When one ends, a red screen covers every display, so you actually take the break.

Made for studying with people

Your group, live

Make a group for your study partner or your class and send the invite code. See who's focusing, who's on a break, and how many minutes everyone did today.

Start at the same second

Start a group tomato and everyone gets a 10-second countdown. Join, focus together, then take the 10-minute group break together.

One timer on every device

Start on your Mac, pause on your Apple Watch, check your iPhone's Lock Screen. They all follow the same timer.
