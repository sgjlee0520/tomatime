Focus together.

or

For people who use Tomatime on an iPhone, Apple Watch or Mac: this PC then follows the same timer. It opens in your browser.

Tomatime lives by the clock, at the bottom right of your screen. Windows hides new icons, so bring the tomato out:

  1. Click the ^ arrow next to the clock.
  2. Drag the tomato down onto the taskbar.

Click the tomato to start a timer or see your group.

Then your group can always invite you to a tomato.
